# Landing illustration

The landing page uses `public/images/norwegian-christmas.jpg`, a locally served,
decorative 1536 × 1024 illustration. It was generated on 2026-10-18 with the
built-in `image_gen` tool, then encoded as JPEG at quality 88 (460,858 bytes).
Interface text is rendered separately as HTML. No external image service is
required at runtime. The mobile layout crops the same scene around the nisse,
snowman, and tree.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production background illustration for a Norwegian
advent-calendar landing page. Create a warm hand-drawn Scandinavian storybook winter scene, wide
landscape composition, delicate ink and colored-pencil details with softly painted paper texture, elegant
and calm. Snow-covered Norwegian countryside at blue-hour twilight, red timber farmhouse with warm golden
windows, dark spruce forest and pale snowy hills. A small traditional Norwegian gårdsnisse wearing a long
red knitted pointed cap, grey wool clothes and mittens, next to a friendly snowman and a spruce Christmas
tree with simple warm lights. Keep the farm nisse, snowman and lit tree as a cohesive recognizable group
across the lower central half so they also fit a narrow mobile crop. Leave the upper half mostly quiet
pale cream wintry sky with subtle snowflakes, to allow real HTML title and body copy to be overlaid
above. Dark evergreen, muted cranberry red, golden window light, warm ivory snow. Charming Norwegian
Christmas, tasteful and welcoming. No text, letters, logos, watermark, UI or borders. Avoid American
Santa Claus, red fur-trimmed Santa suit, candy canes, reindeer sleigh, and commercial American Christmas
imagery. Landscape aspect ratio about 3:2, high quality.
